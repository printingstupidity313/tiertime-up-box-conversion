# tiertime-up-box-conversion
this also aplies to other coumpanies that have simalar printers like affina etc.
a conversion to ender 3 parts as the tiertime coumpany sucks and charges crazy prices still in the works and will be free
the ender 3 parts are much cheaper and will be a perfect replacement keep in mind the motherboard and extruder and all propitary parts will be replaced 
anything with * is optional
the BOM consists of 
jst xh connectors pcb mount or crimp will work about 2pin to 6pin female and male you can get a pack from jaycar or your countrys alternative
wire gp make sure it can handle about 12v 5ah (6 meters) 
genral tools eg: screwdriver, drill, scissors, pliers,wire strippers, idk a hammer etc.
screws just buy a m3-m6 pack about 10 each 
zip ties *
24v 10ah power supply 
motherboard from an ender 3 or as i am using a generic one make sure it can run marlin or klipper and has 4 axis 
you will need about 1.5kgs of filament (way above what you actually need) 
ptfe tubes 
cable chain *
some fans 
motor drivers. 

the print quality will not be as great as a p2s or any modern printer but you cant really expect much from a 10 year old machine

first off start by removing the motherboard and unpluging any cables that are connected. 
remove the side panel that has the filament roll holder (remeber to unplug the cables on the buttons)
you should see a black 3d printed backplate right behind the stepper motor unplug the cables on it and remove it 
with the jst connector (female 4pin) solder (or crimp) the wires in the correct order with the same colour wire so you know what goes where
then connect it to a male jst connector so you can plug it in to the new motherboard 
you should see the cables you unpluged earlier there should be 2 2pin jst connectors those are for your lights you should grab a (2pin jst female connector)
and solder (or crimp) the wires in the corect order (black is ground red is pos) then conect it to your new jst header and you will have functioning lights) 
you will then need to grab the nozzle holder (that is in the 3d prints folder) and connect it to the extruder area. (as shown by the included photo) 
you should then grab your cable chain or if you dont have one just pull the wires to the back making sure that it can reach any part of the bed.
you should then make sure that all of your connectors are in the right order for your new motherboard and you should also have
a few motor drivers make sure they all have heatsinks as they can fail if not and plug those in
you should have the new motheboard and you can instal it in the back and connect your connectors. 
then instal the nesesary softwear and you should be good 

you should also use a raspberry pi i used a zero w and it is running octoprint and on the main board i am runnin marlin 

in total the parts cost around $70 for the entire thing
